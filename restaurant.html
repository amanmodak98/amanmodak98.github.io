The Kitchen

Fire, spice &
patience

Our kitchen is built around two hearts: a blazing clay tandoor and a European range. From one come smoky kebabs, dals simmered overnight and breads blistered in seconds; from the other, risottos, grills and pastas with an Indian soul.

Every chutney is ground in-house, every masala roasted the same morning it is used.
