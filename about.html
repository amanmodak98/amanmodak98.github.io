The Beginning

Built on a
simple belief

The haveli began as a family home by the pilgrim road — a place where travellers to the Ganga were fed before they were asked their names. That kitchen grew into a restaurant, the guest rooms into a hotel, and the courtyard where the family celebrated its own weddings now hosts hundreds of others.

Through every addition, one rule has held: atithi devo bhava — the guest is God.

Milestones

The haveli
through the years

1962

A home by the pilgrim road

The family settles in Santarshah and builds the first wing of the haveli — a courtyard, a kitchen, and rooms that never stayed empty during the melas.

1988

The kitchen opens its doors

What began as free meals for pilgrims becomes a proper bhojanalaya — twelve tables, one tandoor, and a dal makhani people start crossing town for.

2005

The haveli restored

Two years of careful restoration — carved jharokhas rebuilt, the courtyard fountain revived — and the restaurant grows into its present fine-dining form.

2014

The hotel wing

Twenty-eight heritage rooms open upstairs, welcoming pilgrims, yatris and travellers on the Char Dham route.

2019

The Darbar Hall & lawn

The banquet wing opens with the pillared Darbar Hall and the Patram Lawn — and the haveli hosts its first thousand-guest wedding.

Today

Three worlds, one roof

Restaurant, hotel and banquets — run by the fourth generation, with the same open door.
