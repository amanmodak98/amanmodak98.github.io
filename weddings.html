The Journey

Mehndi to
vidaai

Day One

Mehndi &
sangeet

The courtyard turns to colour — cushioned baithaks, henna artists, chaat counters and a dhol that refuses to stop. As dusk falls, the sangeet takes the Darbar Hall stage with full sound and light.

Day Two

Baraat &
pheras

The baraat winds up the lawn to shehnai and fireworks. Under a floral mandap — courtyard or open sky — the pheras are performed by pandits we've trusted for years, with the option of a Ganga-side havan at dawn.

The Finale

Reception &
vidaai

A thousand guests, live counters from our kitchen, and a stage set for photographs that will hang on walls for generations. When the vidaai comes, our team is still there — quietly making sure nothing is left behind.
