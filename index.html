Welcome to Patram

One haveli,
three worlds

Patram Haveli brings together a fine-dining restaurant, a heritage hotel and grand banquet halls under one carved-stone roof in Haridwar. Whether it is an intimate dinner, a restful night after the Ganga aarti, or a wedding of a thousand guests — the haveli holds it all with equal grace.
